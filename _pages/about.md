Hi! I'm **Songsoo Kim**, a radiologist and physician-scientist working at the intersection of **medical-imaging AI and clinical translation**. As AI opens up new methods and new questions for radiology, my goal is to leverage them to enhance the value of medical imaging — and, ultimately, to advance radiology practice and improve patient care.

I'm currently a postdoctoral research fellow at the **[Advanced Imaging and Computational Neuroimaging (AICON) Laboratory](https://sites.google.com/view/snuhradaicon/home)**, Department of Radiology, Seoul National University Hospital, under supervision of Dr. Kyu Sung Choi. Our team brings together clinicians, engineers, and researchers to shape the future of radiology. Before joining SNUH, I completed my diagnostic radiology residency at Gangnam Severance Hospital and my PhD in Biomedical Systems Informatics at Yonsei University College of Medicine.

Outside of work, I stay active at the gym, take long walks around the city with my wife, and chase down good restaurants whenever I can. I also love good discussions that bring new perspectives together — whether over coffee, in conference hallways, or on calls across time zones. These are the moments I genuinely look forward to. If any of this resonates, feel free to reach out!
