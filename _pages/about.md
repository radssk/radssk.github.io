I am **Songsoo Kim, MD, PhD**, a Postdoctoral Research Fellow in the Department of Radiology at Seoul National University Hospital.
My research focuses on **AI for medical imaging and clinical informatics**, with interests spanning radiology report intelligence, multimodal risk prediction, and real-world clinical translation.

I have led and collaborated on projects applying large language models and deep learning to radiology workflows, including report labeling, error detection, and opportunistic screening.
My work has been presented at major international meetings such as **RSNA** and **ECR**, and recognized with awards including the **RSNA Trainee Research Prize**.

My long-term goal is to build reliable, clinically useful AI systems that improve diagnostic quality and patient outcomes.
